Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

BoatKnect is made by Knect. This policy explains what the app and boatknect.com do with your information. In short: an account is optional, we don't track you or show ads, and your location stays on your phone unless you choose to share it.

Location

BoatKnect uses your location to show you on the map, apply the rules where you are, and calculate routes. It is processed on your device. To fetch weather, tide and rain radar, the app sends approximate coordinates of the map area to Open-Meteo and RainViewer; to show nearby ships it sends the map area to aisstream.io. These services receive the request only to answer it.

If you turn on Show my boat, your boat's position and type are shared while the app is open, either with everyone or only with your friends (you choose). It is off by default, and positions are deleted from our server after an hour.

Account (optional)

You can use BoatKnect without an account. If you create one (in the app or on boatknect.com) we store your email address, the display name you choose, and an account ID. Signed in, your boats, saved spots, favourites, launch place and preferences are synced to our server so they appear on your other devices and on the website.

Friends and sharing

You can add friends using an 8-character friend code. Friends see your display name, and routes or spots you send them. Nothing is shared with someone you haven't added.

Community reports

Reports you post (for example a hazard or a free mooring) include the location, type and any note you write, and are visible to other users. They expire automatically. Without an account, reports are linked to a random identifier for your install (we only store a hashed version), so only you can delete them.

Subscriptions

BoatKnect Pro is sold through Apple. Payment and billing are handled by Apple; we don't receive your card or payment details. The app checks with Apple whether your subscription is active.

Where your data is kept

Account and community data is stored with our hosting provider (Supabase, via Lovable Cloud), protected so that only you, or the people you choose, can read it. Without an account, your boats, spots and settings stay only on your device.

Your choices

  • Export your data (JSON) from your account page on boatknect.com.
  • Delete your account in the app (Boat tab → BoatKnect account → Delete account) or on boatknect.com. This removes your account and all its data from our server.
  • Turn off Show my boat at any time.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, analytics SDKs or tracking across apps and websites.
  • We don't sell personal data or share it with third parties for their own use.

Children

BoatKnect is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: info@innovatradecapital.com
